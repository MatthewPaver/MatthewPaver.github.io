How I check the answers.

A useful tool needs more than a convincing answer. Choose a project to see what it checks, where AI helps and which decisions stay with a person.

In the local change-review extension, code checks access before and after a change. AI explains the result; the reviewer owns the decision.

  4. 4. What needs review Do not show an explanation that disagrees with the checked result or its sources

An architect who builds.

I started in data analysis and progressed through data science into solutions architecture over six years at Projecting Success. That path still shapes how I work: understand the decision, check the data, build the system and explain it to the people who will use it.

My experience spans knowledge graphs, AI-assisted workflows and cloud automation, alongside release management, privacy reviews and technical training. I’m interested in what happens after a prototype works: who operates it, how it fails and how people stay in control.

These public projects are independent work. QuickSupply came from an outdated Liverpool supply-teacher booking process; Winchester came from my own property search. They make my engineering choices visible without exposing employer or client systems.
