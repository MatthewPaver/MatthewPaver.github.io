My Decision Contract.

Before AI can influence a decision, I define the source, authority, permitted role, stop condition, owner and retained evidence.

A cloud access review where code owns the verdict and AI is limited to a cited explanation.

Source of truth Policy before and after the proposed change
Deterministic authority Permission evaluation for one sensitive request
Permitted AI role Explain the fixed verdict with claims tied to policy evidence
Abstention condition Withhold the explanation when verdict, claims or citations disagree
Accountable human Cloud security reviewer
Retained decision record Policy diff, granting statement, checks and reviewer decision

What I am trying to prove.

I keep seeing polished AI answers arrive without the checks needed to challenge them. I build the data pipelines, controls, evaluations, interfaces and records around that gap.

The portfolio also shows product delivery beyond AI: turning observed problems into usable workflows, testing the result and documenting where it stops.
