ProjectLens: check the change pack before the board decides.

Change packs and schedules drift apart quietly. ProjectLens loads both in the browser, surfaces the largest conflicts with their source dates, and records the human decision.

Problem The pack says one thing, the schedule says another, and nobody spots it until after the meeting.
Decision Keep everything browser-local and synthetic in the demo, so a stranger can inspect the whole flow.
Proof A one-click Northstar demo on bundled fixtures, with pytest and Playwright suites asserting its outcomes in CI.
Lesson The tool surfaces conflicts; the decision, and its conditions, stay with a named human.
