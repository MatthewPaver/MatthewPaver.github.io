From schedule conflict to an accountable decision

A browser-local evidence desk that checks a change narrative against two schedules and preserves the human decision.

The problem

A change pack can describe a stable finish while its schedules show movement. A reviewer needs the original source, a clear question and an accountable decision record.

What changed

Before. The introduction repeated its pitch while the working comparison required a separate action. Keyword checks could hide factual changes after a narrative edit, and “not on track” could be mistaken for a stability claim.

After. The product landing demonstrates the actual comparison, with selectable finish, constraint and relationship evidence. A replay explains the finish movement, with a static reduced-motion alternative. The full review retains factual changes regardless of wording, handles local negation, and exports original and revised narratives separately. Three priority findings sit beside their source fields; all five Northstar questions remain available. Supplemental context stays optional.

Earlier workflow captures remain available: original workflow and first evidence-desk iteration.

Make the result clear first

The example now names the contradiction in one heading: the finish moves 73 days while the update says unchanged. Schedule formats and spare time are explained in plain language. Source files, exact fields and check time remain expandable. The Northstar review has five findings, with three prioritised.

Complete journey

  1. Load the Northstar example or two supported XER exports and a narrative. Files are parsed in the browser.
  2. Select a finding and inspect its original fields and narrative. Recheck a revision while preserving the first narrative. Ask for evidence, or record the human decision with its owner and rationale.
  3. For conditional approval, supply a condition, owner and due date. Export the evidence-linked record and close the follow-up when complete.
  4. Save a device draft deliberately to resume after reopening. The saved draft includes source files; delete it when finished.

Evidence and practical value

The synthetic Northstar example preserves the 15 December 2027 to 26 February 2028 finish movement: 73 days. Three findings are prioritised; all five detected findings remain in the record. The revised path can save without dismissing any of the three optional public records. Release checks cover Python tests and seven browser suites covering desktop/mobile, source integrity, drafts, storage failure, exports and follow-up.

The value demonstrated here is an inspectable, complete review handoff with clear failure and recovery states. The next test is whether representative users find it useful in their own workflow.

Limits

Supported single-project XER fields and keyword checks only. This is not critical-path, causation, contractual entitlement or approval advice. Browser records are local and unsigned. Representative real exports and planner trials remain the next validation step.
